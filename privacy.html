Privacy Policy

AdLens - Popup Ad Detector. Last updated: 24 October 2026.

The short version

AdLens does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no internet permission, so nothing it sees can leave your phone.

What AdLens sees and why

  • Accessibility service. Android tells AdLens the package name and the type of each window that opens on your screen. AdLens uses this only to show you which app opened a pop-up ad. It does not read text, passwords, notifications or anything else on your screen.
  • Display over other apps. Used only to show the floating icon on top of other apps.
  • Installed apps. AdLens reads the names and icons of installed apps to display them in its lists and to let you choose apps to ignore.
  • Uninstall requests. When you tap Uninstall, AdLens opens Android's own uninstall dialog. You confirm or cancel it there.

What is stored on your phone

The Recently opened list (app package names and the time each app came to the front, kept for 30 days), your list of ignored apps, and your settings (theme, language, floating icon). This stays on your device. You can clear the list in Settings, and uninstalling AdLens deletes everything.

Links and email

"Rate the app", "Share" and this page open in other apps (Google Play, your browser or a sharing app). Those apps have their own privacy policies.

If you send feedback, your email app sends a message to us that includes your email address, what you wrote, and the app version, phone model and Android version filled in at the end (you can delete those before sending). We use it only to reply to you.

Children

AdLens does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.
