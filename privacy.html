1Who we are

AdLens - Popup Ad Detector ("AdLens", "the app") is an Android app made by CloudGTX ("we", "us"). It helps you find which app on your phone is showing pop-up ads. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the AdLens app and to this page.

For the information described in section 5, CloudGTX is the data controller. You can reach us at cloudgtxdesigns@gmail.com.

3Permissions and why

  • Accessibility service. Android tells AdLens the package name and the type of each window that opens on your screen. AdLens uses this only to show you which app opened a pop-up ad, to update the floating icon, and to add the app to your Recently opened list. It does not read text, passwords, messages, notifications or anything else on your screen, and it does not perform actions for you. Before you turn it on, the app explains this and asks you to confirm. You can turn it off at any time in your phone's Accessibility settings.
  • See installed apps. Needed to show app names and icons in the lists, to let you choose apps for your whitelist, and to know which apps to check when you run Scan apps.
  • Request to uninstall apps. When you tap Uninstall, AdLens opens Android's own uninstall dialog. Nothing is removed unless you confirm there.
  • Internet and network state. Used only by Scan apps, to check package names against our adware list.

AdLens does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files, phone calls or SMS.

4Data that stays on your phone

  • Recently opened list: one entry per app that came to the front, with its package name, the last time it opened, the window type, and whether it showed an ad screen.
  • Whitelist: the apps you trust and chose to leave alone.
  • Settings: theme, language and whether the floating icon is on.

This is kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read. We never receive it. You can clear the list in Settings, and uninstalling AdLens deletes all of it. If your phone's Android backup is turned on, Android may include this data in your own Google backup.

5Data that leaves your phone

Scan apps

Only when you start a scan, AdLens sends the package names of the apps you installed (for example com.example.game) to our server, one name per request, to check whether each one is on a list of apps that security researchers reported as adware. System and pre-installed apps, and apps on your whitelist, are not sent. The server answers yes or no for each name.

No account, name, email, device ID, advertising ID or location is sent with them. We do not store the package names you send, keep a history of scans, or link them to you. As with any internet connection, our cloud hosting provider receives your device's IP address in order to deliver the answer (see section 8).

Feedback email

If you choose to send feedback, your own email app sends us a message with your email address, what you wrote, and the app version, phone model and Android version filled in at the end. You can edit or delete any of it before sending. We use it only to reply to you and to fix problems.

6How we use data

  • To show you which app opened a window or a pop-up ad (on your phone).
  • To check your installed apps against the list of reported adware when you run Scan apps.
  • To answer your emails and improve the app based on your feedback.
  • To protect the service from abuse, for example by limiting how the adware list can be read.

Legal bases (EEA and UK users): we process the scan request because you ask for it (performance of the service you requested), and feedback emails on the basis of our legitimate interest in replying to you and improving AdLens. The Accessibility service only runs after you turn it on yourself, and you can turn it off at any time.

7What we never do

  • Sell, rent or trade your data.
  • Show ads, use advertising IDs, or use your data for advertising.
  • Use analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools.
  • Read the content of your screen, your messages, passwords or notifications.
  • Create accounts or profiles about you.

8Sharing and service providers

We use a cloud hosting provider to store the adware list and answer scan requests on our behalf. It acts as our service provider (data processor): it handles the package names and your IP address only to deliver the answer, under its data processing terms, and may not use them for its own purposes.

We do not share your information with anyone else, except if the law requires it (for example a valid court order), or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the app. If AdLens is ever transferred to another owner, this policy will continue to apply to any information involved.

9How long data is kept

  • On your phone: the Recently opened list keeps entries for 30 days. Everything is deleted when you clear it or uninstall AdLens.
  • Scan requests: not stored by us. Our hosting provider may keep short-term technical logs for security and reliability, as described in its own terms.
  • Feedback emails: kept until your question is resolved, then deleted on request.

10Security

Scan requests travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The adware list is protected by access rules that allow checking one name at a time and block anyone from reading or changing the list. Data on your phone stays in the app's private storage. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we keep what we handle to the minimum needed.

11International transfers

Our hosting provider may process scan requests in the United States or other countries where it operates. It uses safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for these transfers.

12Your choices and rights

Choices in the app

  • Don't run Scan apps: nothing is sent.
  • Add apps to your whitelist so they are never listed, shown in the floating icon or scanned.
  • Clear the Recently opened list in Settings.
  • Turn the floating icon off in Settings, or turn Accessibility off in your phone's settings.
  • Uninstall AdLens to delete all data stored by the app.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA and UK, or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Because we do not store scan data or keep accounts, the only personal information we may hold about you is a feedback email. Email us to exercise any of these rights and we will reply within 30 days.

California: we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising, and we do not use sensitive personal information to infer characteristics about you.

13Children

AdLens is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, for example by email, contact us and we will delete it.
