2What AdLens does

AdLens helps you find which app on your phone is showing pop-up ads. With your permission, it notes which app opens a window on your screen, shows it in a floating icon and a Recently opened list, and lets you uninstall apps or add them to your whitelist. Scan apps checks your installed apps against a list of apps that security researchers have reported as adware. AdLens does not block ads and does not remove anything by itself.

3Scan results and uninstalling

  • Results are information, not a guarantee. The adware list is compiled from public reports by third-party security researchers. It may be incomplete, out of date or, in rare cases, wrong. An app that is not flagged is not guaranteed to be safe, and a flagged app may have changed since it was reported.
  • You decide what to remove. AdLens never uninstalls apps itself. When you tap Uninstall, Android's own dialog asks you to confirm. Before removing an app, make sure you do not need it and back up any data it holds.
  • The floating icon and Recently opened list show which app was in front when a window opened. This usually points to the app behind a pop-up ad, but it is not proof.
